    Welcome to Darooghe

    Darooghe checks every component on a frame against your team's design system and shows you exactly what's off. Two steps to your first audit:
    1
    Select a frame
    Click a frame or section on the canvas. Hold Shift to pick several.
    2
    Run the audit
    You get a compliance score and a list of issues, each with its fix.
    3
    Fix and audit again
    Click an issue to jump to its layer. With no issues left, the frame is verified for handoff.

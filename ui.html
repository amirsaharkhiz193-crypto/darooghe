    Changelog

    Welcome to Darooghe

    Darooghe checks every component on a frame against your team's design system and shows you exactly what's off. Two steps to your first audit:
    1
    Register your library
    Open your design system library file, run Darooghe and press Settings → Register this file.
    1
    Pick what to audit
    On the Audit tab, choose a frame or section from the Target list — or simply click one on the canvas and it is picked up automatically.
    2
    Run & fix
    Press Run audit. You'll get a score and a list of issues — click any row to highlight that layer on the canvas, fix it, then Re-audit to confirm.
